About Southwest Chapter

The Southwest Chapter of the Florida Association of Mortgage Professionals brings mortgage professionals together through relationships, education, advocacy, and meaningful industry connections.

We believe that a stronger mortgage industry begins with an informed, connected, and engaged professional community.
